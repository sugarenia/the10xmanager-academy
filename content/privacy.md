## What I collect and why

**Contact form.** Your name, email and message, so I can reply. The form is processed by Netlify, which hosts this site, and forwarded to my inbox. Legal basis: your request, and my legitimate interest in answering it. I keep these messages for as long as we're in touch, and delete them within two years of our last exchange unless we start working together.

**Newsletter.** Your email address, so I can send you the newsletter. It's stored by beehiiv, which sends the emails and records opens and clicks so I can see what's useful. Legal basis: your consent, which you confirm by email. Every issue has an unsubscribe link, and unsubscribing stops all emails.

**Booking a call.** If you book through my calendar link, Google Calendar receives the details you enter, and I use them only to hold the call.

**Server logs.** Netlify keeps standard technical logs (such as IP address and browser) for security and to keep the site running. I don't use them to identify you.

## What I don't do

No analytics, no advertising, no tracking cookies, and I never sell or share your data. The site saves your light or dark theme choice in your browser; it never leaves your device.

## Where your data goes

Netlify, beehiiv and Google are based in the US. They transfer data under the EU's Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.
