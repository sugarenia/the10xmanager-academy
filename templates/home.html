// why I do this

Promoted on a Friday. Managing on Monday.

Most of us became managers by accident. Someone left, the team grew, and the person who knew the system best was asked to step up. We said yes, often reluctantly, because it felt like the right thing to do.

The support never came. Junior engineers get onboarding, documentation and code reviews. New managers get a title and a calendar full of 1:1s.

I work with managers, and with the companies that promote them, so nobody has to learn this job alone.

How a team coaching programme works

  1. Step 1 · kickoff

    Scoping call

    You and I talk through your team, your managers and what you want to be different by the end.

  2. Step 2 · first weeks

    Self-assessment

    Each manager maps their strengths and blind spots and sets goals with me, privately.

  3. Step 3 · throughout

    Coaching

    1:1 sessions on their real situations, plus group sessions where managers learn from each other.

  4. Step 4 · at the end

    Insights report

    You receive anonymised patterns across the team and where to invest next. Individual sessions stay confidential.
